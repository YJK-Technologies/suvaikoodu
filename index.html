The Art of Suvaikoodu

Patience Measured in Golden Flames & Pure A2 Ghee

True South Indian and royal palace mithai cannot be rushed by industrial mixers. Inside our kitchen, every batch begins before sunrise with single-origin ingredients and quiet reverence for heirloom craft.

01. Churned A2 Bilona Cow Ghee

Cultured from curd rather than cream for an unmistakable nutty aroma and clean, melt-in-mouth richness.

02. Grade-A Pampore Kashmiri Saffron

Whole crimson stigmas steeped gently in warm milk and cardamom syrup—never synthetic food color or essence.

03. Hand-Rolled & Baked Fresh Every Morning

Both our traditional mithai and fusion patisserie cakes leave our ovens and brass trays the very day you celebrate.
